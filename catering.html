Catering

Seasonal food from regional producers, planned around your space, your guests and the shape of the evening.

Occasions

  • Private dinners

    A seated menu cooked in your home or a venue you choose, for small groups.

  • Openings and exhibitions

    Finger food and drinks for vernissages, studio openings and launches, served so guests can keep moving.

  • Company and team events

    Lunches, workshops and celebrations, with clear pricing per guest.

  • Celebrations

    Birthdays, anniversaries and garden parties, from buffet to family-style sharing plates.

From enquiry to evening

  1. Enquiry

    Send the date, location and number of guests. You'll hear back within two working days.

  2. Menu proposal

    A suggested menu and quote, adjusted for dietary needs and allergies.

  3. Planning

    Timings, layout of food and service, equipment and any staff needed.

  4. The event

    Setup, service and clearing away, so you can spend the time with your guests.

Good to know

Menus change with the seasons and are written for each event. Vegetarian, vegan and allergy-friendly options are always available.

Please enquire at least three weeks ahead for larger events.
