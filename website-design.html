Website design

Websites that tell visitors who you are within seconds, load quickly on any phone, and stay easy for you to update.

What I make

  • Portfolio and studio sites

    For architects, designers, photographers and makers. The work is the focus; the layout stays out of its way.

  • Small business websites

    Clear pages for what you offer, where you are and how to book. Set up for search engines and Google Maps.

  • Landing pages

    A single page for a launch, an event or a campaign, delivered in days rather than weeks.

  • Redesigns

    A fresh look and structure for a site that has grown messy, keeping the content and search rankings you already have.

How a project runs

  1. Brief

    A first call about your goals, audience and budget. You get a written proposal with a fixed price.

  2. Structure

    A site map and page layouts, agreed before any visual design starts.

  3. Design and build

    Typography, colour and imagery, built as a fast, accessible site you review as it grows.

  4. Launch

    Your domain connected, a short handover session, and support for the first month.

Included in every site

Responsive layouts for phone, tablet and desktop. Accessible contrast and keyboard navigation. Basic search engine setup. Secure HTTPS hosting. A contact form that lands in your inbox.

Hosting can run free on GitHub Pages for static sites, or on the provider you already use.
