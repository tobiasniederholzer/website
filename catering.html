Occasions

  • Private dinners

    A seated menu cooked in your home or a venue of your choice, for small groups.

  • Openings and exhibitions

    Finger food and drinks for vernissages, studio openings and launches, served so guests keep moving.

  • Company and team events

    Lunches, workshops and celebrations, with clear pricing per guest.

  • Celebrations

    Birthdays, anniversaries and garden parties, from buffet to family-style sharing plates.

From enquiry to evening

  1. Enquiry

    Send the date, location and number of guests. You'll hear back within two working days.

  2. Menu proposal

    A suggested menu and quote, adjusted for dietary needs and allergies.

  3. Planning

    Timings, food layout, service, equipment and any staff needed.

  4. The event

    Setup, service and clearing away, so you can spend the time with your guests.

Good to know

Menus change with the seasons and are written for each event.

  • Vegetarian and vegan options always available
  • Allergies and dietary needs taken seriously
  • Regional, seasonal ingredients
  • Please enquire three weeks ahead for larger events
